# Code of conduct

raea's issues, pull requests, reviews and other project-managed community spaces
should let people discuss and improve the work without harassment. This applies
to maintainers, contributors and people using automated or AI-assisted tools.
Participants remain responsible for the material they submit through those tools.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss the code, evidence and ideas respectfully. Technical disagreement is
  welcome; personal attacks and assumptions about another person's motives are not.
- Give actionable feedback, accept corrections and respect requests to stop a
  conversation or move it out of a thread.
- Make room for different experience levels, backgrounds and communication styles.
- Respect privacy. Share only logs, screenshots and other information you have
  permission to publish, with credentials and personal information removed.

Harassment, discriminatory insults, threats, sexual harassment, unwanted repeated
contact, disclosure of private information, deliberate disruption and retaliation
against someone raising a concern are unacceptable.

## Reporting a concern

For content on GitHub, use the content menu's **Report content** action where it
is available. Choose **Report to repository admins** if GitHub offers it.
Maintainer reporting depends on repository settings and the reporter's access.
For abuse covered by GitHub's rules, use **Report abuse to GitHub Support** or
[GitHub's abuse-reporting route](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
GitHub handles those reports under its own policies; it is not the project's
appeal body for every technical or moderation disagreement.

A separate private maintainer inbox for conduct reports has not been designated
in this repository. If GitHub's maintainer-reporting option is unavailable, ask
for a private contact without posting the incident, personal information or
accusations in a public issue. Maintainers can document an additional contact
after confirming who will handle it. Use [the security policy](SECURITY.md) for vulnerabilities,
not as a general conduct-report channel.

Include links, the relevant context and your preferred way to be contacted when
reporting privately. Do not retaliate, publicly investigate the reporter or
pressure someone to disclose an incident.

## Moderation

Repository maintainers may clarify expectations, ask participants to change
behavior, close or remove disruptive content, restrict interactions, or block
continued participation as appropriate to severity and repetition. A technical
disagreement or a rejected contribution alone is not misconduct.

Maintainers should consider context and available evidence, protect sensitive
information, explain decisions when doing so does not compromise privacy, and
allow a participant to provide relevant additional context privately through the
same reporting route. A maintainer involved in a concern should seek an uninvolved
reviewer where one is available. This policy does not promise a response deadline
or confidentiality beyond what the chosen reporting service and process provide.
